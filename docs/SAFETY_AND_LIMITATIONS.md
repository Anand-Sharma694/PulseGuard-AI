# Safety and Limitations

- This is an educational software prototype.
- The training dataset is synthetic.
- Accuracy, precision, recall and F1 are not clinical performance measures.
- The system does not diagnose disease or prescribe treatment.
- Thresholds 60/100 BPM are demonstration thresholds from the original project brief, not individualized medical advice.
- Real deployment would require validated sensors, clinically appropriate datasets, security, privacy controls, monitoring, formal verification and regulatory review.
