# Product Walkthrough

## For a non-technical viewer
1. Open the home page.
2. Read “What happens when you press Start?”
3. Click Create my dashboard or Try demo account.
4. On Dashboard, click Explain this screen.
5. Start monitoring.
6. Click Normal, High and Low demo buttons.
7. Read the AI explanation after each result.
8. Open AI explained to understand the two models.
9. Open My history to see stored events and export CSV.
10. Open How it works to see the complete system architecture.

## For a recruiter/interviewer
Ask the candidate to explain:
- Why Random Forest?
- Why add Isolation Forest?
- What are the six engineered features?
- How is risk score calculated?
- How is explainability generated?
- Why use SQLite?
- How would sensor data enter the system?
- How would the system change for production?
- Why are synthetic metrics not presented as clinical accuracy?
