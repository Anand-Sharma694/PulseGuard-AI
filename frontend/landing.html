SIMPLE EXPLANATION

What happens when you press Start?

You don't need to understand machine learning to understand the result.

01

Collect

The system receives a stream of heart-rate values. Right now the project can simulate the stream on a laptop.

02

Understand

It calculates recent average, minimum, maximum, variation and sudden changes.

03

Analyze

Two ML models look for patterns: one classifies patterns and one searches for unusual behavior.

04

Explain

Instead of only saying “alert”, PulseGuard tells the viewer what changed and why the pattern was flagged.

AI WITHOUT THE JARGON

Two AI models, one clearer answer.

Random Forest learns examples of normal and unusual demonstration patterns.

Isolation Forest learns what a normal window looks like and flags patterns that stand apart.

PulseGuard combines those signals with transparent threshold rules to produce a status, confidence, risk score and explanation.
